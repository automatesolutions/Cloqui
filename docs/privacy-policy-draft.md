## The short version

- Everything you put in Cloqui is private by default. Only people you invite can see it.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train AI.
- Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. It is **not** end-to-end encrypted: our systems
  can technically read it so they can show it to the people you share it with.
- You can download your data or delete your account at any time from Account and security.

## What we collect

**Account details**: your name, email address, password (stored hashed, never readable), and,
if you turn it on, your two-step sign-in setup.

**What you create**: videos and their thumbnails, captions and comments; chat messages and
attachments; tasks, comments and files; live room details. Live calls themselves aren't recorded.

**Usage and security data**: when you sign in, the device and browser, IP address, and
who viewed a video and when (shown to the video's owner). Workspace admins see an audit log of
admin actions.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use cookies only to keep you signed in.

## Why we use it

- To run Cloqui: store your content and show it to the people you share it with.
- To keep it secure: sign-in, two-step sign-in, rate limits, and audit logs.
- To send email you'd expect: invites, sign-in links and account confirmations. Notifications
  stay inside Cloqui; we don't email them.
