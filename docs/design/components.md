## Changes made to shadcn defaults

- `button`: sizes are `default` 44, `sm` 36 + invisible 4 px hit padding, `lg` 52, `icon` 44. Adds `loading` (spinner, keeps width) and a `danger` (solid) variant. Press = `scale(.98)`.
- `input` / `textarea`: 44 px, `input-border` (3:1), accent focus outline.
- `badge`: `private`, `workspace`, `attention`, `live` variants matching the AccessChip.
- Menus (`dropdown-menu`, `select`): items 36 px with a pointer, 44 px on touch (`pointer-coarse:`).
- Overlays: Cloqui scrim + 4 px blur. Dialogs fade and slide 8 px instead of zooming.
